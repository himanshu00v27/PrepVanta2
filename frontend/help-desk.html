Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register on PrepVanta?

Go to the Sign Up page, enter your full name, choose a unique username and register with your email or phone number. You'll instantly receive an 8-digit PrepVanta user ID.

I forgot my username — can I still log in?

Yes. Log in using the email or phone number you registered with; your username and user ID stay linked to that account.

Does the compiler support every language shown?

JavaScript runs directly in your browser today. Support for compiling the other listed languages server-side is on the roadmap.

How is my topic-wise progress calculated?

Every question you attempt across Topics, Reasoning and Aptitude updates your dashboard automatically, broken down by subject.

Can other users find my profile?

Yes — other learners can search for you by your username or your 8-digit user ID from the Find Users page.
